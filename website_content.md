# GODIX Website Content Architecture

## Purpose

This document defines the complete website information architecture, page strategy, section structure, and content model for GODIX as an industry-led developer community and learning platform. It is intentionally content-first and does not include implementation code, styling, animation, or UI execution details.

---

## Information Architecture Logic

The website is structured around a learner journey that moves from awareness to trust to enrollment to outcomes.

- Home: brand story, value proposition, trust signals, and conversion entry point
- About: company story, mission, vision, values, and philosophy
- Programs: educational offerings, structure, outcomes, and learning methodology
- Roadmap: progression from beginner to career-ready engineer
- Projects: experiential learning, project workflow, and job-readiness proof
- Mentors: industry expertise, mentorship model, and community leadership
- Testimonials: success stories and social proof
- FAQ: enrollment, curriculum, fees, outcomes, and support questions
- Contact: lead generation and community engagement
- Privacy Policy: legal and data handling information
- Terms and Conditions: user rules and platform terms

# FINAL WEBSITE CONTENT STRATEGY SUMMARY

## Brand Positioning

GODIX should be positioned as an industry-led developer community focused on practical engineering growth, project execution, mentorship, and career readiness.

## Narrative Principle

The website should tell a story of skill building through action, not passive instruction.

## Conversion Principle

Every page should move the visitor closer to one of three outcomes:

- Explore programs
- Learn the experience model
- Connect with the team or apply

## Tone of Voice

- Practical
- Encouraging
- Professional
- Community-driven
- Outcome-focused
- Modern but grounded

## Editorial Rule

The site should feel like a serious engineering community, not a traditional classroom or academy brochure.

## Recommended Priority Order

1. Home
2. Programs
3. Roadmap
4. Mentors
5. Testimonials
6. FAQ
7. Contact
8. About
9. Projects
10. Privacy Policy and Terms

This content architecture is designed to support a website that feels credible, modern, and aligned with the mission of building capable developers through community, project work, and industry-informed mentorship.
